**Facebook Post — Body Doubling:**

Have you ever noticed that you're somehow MORE productive in a busy coffee shop than you are alone at home with complete silence?

That's not a coincidence. It's called body doubling — and it's one of the most well-documented, consistently effective ADHD strategies that almost no one talks about.

Here's the basic idea: ADHD brains have lower baseline dopamine activity. When another person is nearby and working, it activates social awareness circuits that inadvertently improve sustained attention. You're not being watched. You're not being judged. Something in your nervous system just... switches on.

The good news: you can use this even if you work from home alone.

→ Focusmate.com offers free virtual body doubling sessions with real people
→ "Study with me" videos on YouTube work almost as well
→ Working from a café once or twice a week is body doubling in its original form

**Instagram Caption — RSD:**

One piece of critical feedback in a great performance review → can't eat, can't sleep, replaying it 40 times.

A read receipt with no reply → convinced they hate you for 3 hours.

A slightly flat tone in a text → spending the afternoon wondering what you did wrong.

This is Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria. And it's neurological — not dramatic, not "too sensitive," not a choice.

RSD is what happens when the ADHD brain's emotional regulation circuits fire disproportionately to perceived rejection or criticism. The pain is real. The intensity is real. The story your brain tells you about the situation? Not always real.

What helps:
→ Naming it ("this is RSD, not reality")
→ The HALT check (Hungry/Anxious/Lonely/Tired?)
→ The 30-minute delay before responding to anything
→ A trusted person for reality-checks

Full breakdown on the blog — including why it gets misdiagnosed, what medication options exist, and how to communicate your RSD to people who love you.
